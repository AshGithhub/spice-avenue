15+ Years of Culinary Heritage
Our Tradition

Crafting Culinary Memories in Hyderabad

Founded in the heart of Hyderabad, Spice Avenue brings together age-old regal recipes, hand-picked whole spices, and modern culinary expertise.

From slow-cooked dum biryanis sealed with hand-kneaded dough to clay-oven tandoori delicacies, every dish is crafted with fresh local ingredients, cold-pressed oils, and uncompromising standards of hygiene.
